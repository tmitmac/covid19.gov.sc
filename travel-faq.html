Historical information — not current advice

This page records information published during the Square Country COVID-19 response. Restrictions, eligibility rules, telephone services and instructions below are no longer in force. For current advice, contact the Bureau of Biosecurity and Disease Prevention.

Do I need a COVID-19 test to enter Square Country now?

This archive does not publish current border requirements. Check the present immigration and border guidance before travel.

What was managed isolation?

Managed isolation was a controlled accommodation arrangement used for some arrivals during the pandemic.

Were there domestic travel restrictions?

During some higher response levels, non-essential inter-district travel was restricted.

Can I use a certificate from this site?

No. The certificate services and links published during the emergency programme have been retired.

Where are previous travel announcements?

See the government announcements and border reopening archive for historical information.
