Historical information — not current advice

This page records information published during the Square Country COVID-19 response. Restrictions, eligibility rules, telephone services and instructions below are no longer in force. For current advice, contact the Bureau of Biosecurity and Disease Prevention.

Common symptoms

COVID-19 symptoms can vary. Common symptoms reported during the public health response included:

  • Fever or chills
  • Cough, sore throat or congestion
  • Fatigue, headache or muscle aches
  • Shortness of breath
  • New loss of taste or smell
  • Nausea or diarrhoea

Symptoms alone cannot reliably distinguish COVID-19 from other respiratory infections.

Seek urgent medical help

Severe difficulty breathing, persistent chest pain, new confusion, or difficulty staying awake can indicate a medical emergency. Seek emergency assistance through your local medical services.

Previously published advice

During the emergency period, anyone with symptoms was asked to stay home, arrange testing when appropriate, and follow the isolation rules applying at that time. These rules are preserved in the historical archive.
