A permanent historical record

covid19.gov.sc was the central online information service for the Government of Square Country’s response to COVID-19. It published health advice, travel requirements, public health orders, service information and official announcements between 2020 and 2023.

Following the end of the pandemic emergency programme, the website was preserved to support historical reference, transparency and research.

Status of the content

  • Last editorial update: 21 July 2023.
  • Status: Read-only archive.
  • Emergency services: Closed.
  • Forms and applications: Not accepted through this archive.
  • Legal guidance: Historical and not authoritative for current requirements.

A note about this project

Square Country and this government COVID-19 archive are fictional. The content, historic milestones and public policy descriptions are created for a simulated government website and should not be used as real medical, legal, or travel advice.
