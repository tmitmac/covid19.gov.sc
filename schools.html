Historical information — not current advice

This page records information published during the Square Country COVID-19 response. Restrictions, eligibility rules, telephone services and instructions below are no longer in force. For current advice, contact the Bureau of Biosecurity and Disease Prevention.

Education during COVID-19

Schools, childcare centres and tertiary institutions adjusted their operations according to government response levels and health guidance. At times this included remote learning, staggered attendance and temporary closures.

Support for families

Education providers communicated learning expectations, access to devices and wellbeing support directly with families. Arrangements differed across institutions and over time.

Return to normal operation

Emergency nationwide education measures were discontinued as the response ended. Current school policies are the responsibility of education and health authorities.
