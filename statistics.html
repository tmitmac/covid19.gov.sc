Historical information — not current advice

This page records information published during the Square Country COVID-19 response. Restrictions, eligibility rules, telephone services and instructions below are no longer in force. For current advice, contact the Bureau of Biosecurity and Disease Prevention.

Historical reporting overview

Throughout the pandemic, the Ministry of Health published regular case counts, testing volumes, hospital admissions and vaccination progress. Daily reporting was progressively reduced as emergency surveillance ended.

Statistics archive closed

The figures and charts originally hosted here were frozen after the final statistical bulletin. No live case counts are maintained on this site.

Interpreting the data

Definitions, testing access and reporting practices changed during the pandemic. Historical figures should not be compared across periods without considering these differences. This demonstration archive intentionally does not invent cumulative case or death totals.
