Looking after your wellbeing

The pandemic affected the wellbeing of many people. Isolation, loss, uncertainty and disruption to work or study can all cause stress. This page preserves general strategies shared during the response.

  • Maintain regular contact with family, friends or people you trust.
  • Keep a manageable daily routine and rest.
  • Take breaks from distressing news and social media.
  • Stay physically active when possible.
  • Seek support from a health professional if distress is persistent or overwhelming.

Finding help

The emergency counselling telephone lines and short-term funding programmes advertised in 2020–2022 are no longer maintained here. Contact current healthcare or emergency services if you need urgent assistance.
